Privacy first

We keep the connection, not the notes.

No profiles. No ads. No mining your notes. Your vault lives in Obsidian, and in the GitHub repository you connect — not as our product.

What we store

Only what it takes to know your devices are connected and in sync.

  • Connected devicesHow many devices are linked, and the name you gave each one.
  • PlatformmacOS, Windows, Linux, Android, or iOS — plus the plugin version on that device.
  • Sync healthLast seen, last successful sync, sync count, and how many files moved. Counts, not the notes themselves.

Access tokens are stored hashed. GitHub App secrets are encrypted. MCP never stores note bodies. Operators cannot open another person’s vault.
